Mailburstly — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026 · Applies to https://mailburstly.openehsaan.com

What Mailburstly is

Mailburstly is an email outreach manager. It lets you connect your own Gmail (and other) mailboxes so that email campaigns you author are sent from your address, and replies land where they belong — your inbox.

What we access when you connect a mailbox

To send, read replies, and keep your connection alive, Mailburstly requests:

  • OpenID, email, profile — to identify you and associate the mailbox with your account.
  • Gmail ("modify") access — so Mailburstly can send campaign messages from your address, read incoming replies (headers and bodies) to classify them, and apply labels or mark threads read to keep your inbox consistent with what the tool did.

What we store

  • Mail metadata for messages the tool sent or classified: sender, recipient, subject line, labels/folder names, and message identifiers. We do not persist the bodies of your regular (non-campaign) mail.
  • OAuth tokens for your connected mailbox, stored encrypted at rest. Tokens are used only to operate the mailbox on your behalf and are deleted when you disconnect.
  • Account data you create in the app: contacts, campaign and template content, and operational logs (e.g. that a send happened).

What we do not do

  • We do not read, index, or train on your personal email beyond what sending/reply-classification requires.
  • We do not sell or share your mailbox data with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not send email from your mailbox outside campaigns you configure, or outside sending windows and limits you can see.

Removing access

Disconnect the mailbox in Mailburstly — this deletes our stored tokens and account link immediately. You can also revoke Mailburstly's access at any time in your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) without affecting other apps.
