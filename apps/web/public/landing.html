Email outreach manager

Mail Burstly — cold email campaigns from your own mailbox.

Mail Burstly is an email outreach manager for teams that run outbound campaigns.

You connect the Gmail or Outlook mailbox you already own, write a multi-step sequence, and Mail Burstly sends it from that address on the schedule you set. Every reply is read, classified and filed in one inbox, while opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes roll up into analytics — and a campaign pauses itself the moment deliverability slips.

What Mail Burstly does

One place to run, watch and answer a campaign — without a third party sending mail on your behalf.

Connect your own mailboxes

Link Gmail, Outlook or any IMAP/SMTP mailbox. Per-mailbox health and daily quota are visible, so you send from the inboxes that still deliver.

Import leads, build lists

Upload a CSV or a sheet, map the columns to variables, dedupe on import and keep contacts in named, reusable lists.

Write multi-step sequences

Build a campaign step by step: subject, body, delay in days, A/Z variants, and a preview rendered exactly as the recipient will see it.

Personalise every send

Merge variables, fallback values, spintax and conditionals are resolved at send time, so a lead never receives a raw token.

Send on your own schedule

Send windows with timezones, daily caps, pacing and jitter between mailboxes, plus stop conditions for replies, bounces and out-of-office.

Work every reply in one inbox

Replies from every connected mailbox land in one thread view, attributed to the lead and the campaign, and you answer them without leaving the app.

Classify replies automatically

Interested, not interested, question, out-of-office, unsubscribe — rules decide, with an optional model behind them and a safe fallback when it is unsure.

Analytics that explain themselves

Sent, contacted, replies and conversions per campaign, step and variant, with a filterable activity log and CSV export.

Auto-pause on poor health

A campaign pauses itself when bounce rate, reply rate, open rate, unsubscribe rate or mailbox health crosses a threshold — and records why.

Webhooks and a public API

Push events to your own systems and read the same data from a key-authenticated REST API.

How it works

The loop is the same for every campaign, and it is deliberately boring: write it, watch it, fix what the data tells you.

  1. Connect a mailbox Authorise the inbox you want to send from.
  2. Import your leads Upload a list and map the fields you want to personalise.
  3. Build the sequence Write the steps, set delays, preview and send yourself a test.
  4. Launch it Pick send windows and limits, then let the scheduler run it.
  5. Work the replies Classified replies wait in the inbox; analytics show what to improve.

Why Mail Burstly asks for Google account access

When you connect a mailbox, Mail Burstly uses the Gmail API with modify permission. That is the narrowest scope that lets it do the job:

  • Send the campaign messages from the address you connected.
  • Read the replies to those messages so they can be classified and filed.
  • Label or mark those threads read, so your inbox matches what the tool did.

It also requests your OpenID, email and profile to identify the account. Mail Burstly does not read, index or sell your personal mail, and it never sends anything outside a campaign you configured. The privacy policy has the full detail, including how to disconnect a mailbox and revoke access.

Who uses Mail Burstly

Mail Burstly is an internal tool for outbound operators and the teams they run campaigns for. It is operated by Ehsaan Tech.
